LLM Powered Autonomous Agents

The field of autonomous agents has grown rapidly with the power of Large Language Models.
In June 2023, Lilian Weng published a seminal blog post outlining LLM-powered autonomous agents, structuring them into planning, memory, and tool use components.
In March 2023, Toran Bruce Richards released AutoGPT, demonstrating self-directed task loops that execute complex multi-step objectives autonomously.
Later in 2023, researchers from Stanford and Google published the Generative Agents paper, creating a virtual town sandbox where 25 autonomous agents lived and interacted.
By 2024, multi-agent orchestrations like CrewAI and Microsoft's AutoGen emerged, shifting focus from single-agent loops to collaborative agent networks.
Autonomous agents represent the next major shift in AI application design.
