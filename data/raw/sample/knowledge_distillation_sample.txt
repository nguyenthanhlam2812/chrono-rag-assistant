Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network

Knowledge distillation is a key model compression technique.
In 2015, Geoffrey Hinton, Oriol Vinyals, and Jeff Dean popularized the concept of Knowledge Distillation, showing that dark knowledge from a large teacher ensemble could be transferred into a single smaller student model using softened logits.
In 2019, Victor Sanh et al. released DistilBERT, compressing BERT by 40% while retaining 97% of its performance.
In 2020, Jiao et al. introduced TinyBERT, performing transformer-layer distillation at both pre-training and fine-tuning stages.
By 2023, knowledge distillation became crucial for migrating reasoning capabilities from API-only giants like GPT-4 into smaller open weights models like LLaMA-based models.
Distillation remains a primary path for deploying high-performance models to edge devices.
